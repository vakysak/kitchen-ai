Laser Tech - bezklejowe łączenie obrzeża z płytą, ideal-

PUR Glue - poliuretanowy klej o niewidocznej spoinie, 
podwyższonej trwałości, odpornej na wilgoć, parę 
i temperaturę. Idealnie sprawdza się kuchniach.

Why Extom?
solid workmanship and proven materials,
modern production technologies,
a wide range of styles and solutions,
full customization to meet individual customer needs.
Technologies that make a difference
Laser Tech - glue-free bonding of the edge to the board, perfect-
ly smooth edges and high resistance to moisture and tempera-
ture.  Ideal for kitchens.
PUR Glue - Polyurethane adhesive with an invisible joint, 
enhanced durability, and resistance to moisture, steam, and 
temperature. Ideal for kitchens.
Functionality in every detail
A wide selection of handles, drawer systems, hinges, lighting, and 
accessories that enhance everyday comfort and highlight the 
style of the kitchen.
Customization without compromise
We design furniture tailored to your space and lifestyle. We offer 
non-standard dimensions, ﬁnishes, and color options to make the 
most of the available space.
